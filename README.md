# Customer Churn & Retention Strategy

### Data-Driven Customer Risk, Churn Analysis & Retention Planning

An end-to-end **Power BI customer analytics project** designed to analyze customer churn, identify high-risk customer segments, prioritize retention opportunities, and translate customer insights into actionable retention strategies.

---

## 📌 Business Problem

Customer churn can significantly impact revenue and long-term customer value. However, identifying **which customers are most at risk, why they are at risk, and where retention efforts should be prioritized** is often challenging.

This project addresses the problem by analyzing customer characteristics across:

- Churn behavior
- Customer risk segments
- Engagement status
- Customer value segments
- Age groups
- Geography
- Retention priority

The objective is to move from **descriptive churn analysis to actionable retention decisions**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Measure overall customer churn and retention
- Identify customer groups with elevated churn risk
- Segment customers based on risk and value
- Identify high-priority customers requiring intervention
- Analyze churn patterns across geography, age and product engagement
- Develop a structured retention priority framework
- Translate analysis into practical business recommendations

# 🔎 Key Business Insights

### 1. Churn is a meaningful customer-retention challenge

The dashboard identifies a **20.37% churn rate**, indicating that approximately one in five customers is classified as churned.

### 2. High-value customers require particular attention

The analysis shows differences in churn behavior across customer value segments, making customer value an important factor when prioritizing retention efforts.

### 3. Customer risk is highly concentrated

The risk segmentation framework highlights **Medium Risk, Low Risk, Churned-Historical, High Risk and Critical Risk** customer groups, allowing retention efforts to be differentiated rather than treating all customers equally.

### 4. A relatively small high-risk group can require disproportionate attention

The dashboard identifies **439 high critical-risk customers**, providing a focused group for targeted intervention.

### 5. Customer characteristics influence retention priorities

Risk and retention patterns vary across:

- Geography
- Age group
- Engagement status
- Customer value
- Product relationship

This supports a **segmented retention strategy rather than a one-size-fits-all approach**.

---

# 💡 Retention Strategy Framework

Based on the analysis, customers can be prioritized according to **risk + customer value + engagement**.

| Customer Situation | Recommended Focus |
|---|---|
| Critical / High Risk + High Value | Immediate personalized intervention |
| High Risk + Medium Value | Targeted retention offers |
| Churned-Historical | Win-back campaigns |
| Low Risk + High Value | Loyalty and relationship-building |
| Medium Risk | Proactive engagement |
| Lower-risk customers | Maintain engagement and monitor |

The objective is to allocate retention resources where they are most likely to create business value.
